# Yuri Norstein: Hedgehog in the Fog and Multiplane Depth

## Overview
Yuri Norstein directed *Hedgehog in the Fog* (1975), with visual artwork by Francheska Yarbusova. Layered cut-outs and a multiplane apparatus created spatial depth; haze and concealment control attention. Backlog FS-209. The creative role of Yarbusova must not be erased.

## Influenced By
- Cut-out and multiplane animation: separated layers, independent motion, soft depth.
- Drawn and textured surfaces: tactile handmade appearance rather than photographic realism.

## Influenced To
- Layered digital animation as a compatible modern application, without asserting direct influence on individual works.

## Relations
- [Arkhip Kuindzhi](../kuindzhi_moonlit_dnieper/README.md): selected points of light amid darkness.
- [René Lalique](../lalique_dragonfly_jewellery/README.md): optical layers and partial visibility.

## Style Requirements
- **Planes:** Readable foreground, middle and distant layers with overlaps.
- **Occlusion:** Fog partially hides recognizable forms rather than covering everything equally.
- **Parallax:** In animation, camera motion moves near/far layers differently.
- **Softness:** Distance loses contrast while near forms remain legible.
- **Quiet focal scale:** A small identifiable subject inhabits a larger, mysterious field.
- **Texture:** A static study shows gentle mottling or cut-paper character.
- **Originality:** Do not reproduce film characters, forest silhouettes or specific film frames.
