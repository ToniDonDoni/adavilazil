# German Expressionist Poster

## Origin
German Expressionism and expressive woodcut printing, early 20th century.

## Background
Printmaking turns psychological tension into simplified, distorted shapes. The rules below describe a reproducible poster interpretation, not a universal definition of the movement.

## Style Requirements
- **GE-01 Light:** Use large, hard-edged black shadows.
- **GE-02 Color:** Limit the palette to black, paper tones and at most one accent.
- **GE-03 Form:** Distort at least one recognizable object into sharp angles.
- **GE-04 Line:** Show jagged, carved-looking strokes inside the subject.
- **GE-05 Space:** Tilt or compress architectural or landscape perspective.
- **GE-06 Composition:** Build tension with a dominant diagonal.
- **GE-07 Texture:** Show visibly uneven ink or woodcut grain.
- **GE-08 Mood:** Convey unease using pose, shadow or spatial pressure.
