# Psychedelic Poster

## Overview
A 1960s San Francisco counterculture concert-poster language that uses curling ornament, visually vibrating color and distorted text or imagery. The digital interpretation emphasizes optical movement rather than treating the effect as a uniform color filter.

## Influenced By
- Art Nouveau: organic, undulating forms and decorative lettering.
- Optical Art: spatial effects, patterned geometry and color vibration.

## Influenced To
- Later concert-poster and music-graphics practice: the enduring vocabulary of swirling contours, optically active color and ornamental lettering.

## Relations
- [German Expressionist poster](../german_poster/README.md): both reshape the subject for emotional intensity, but psychedelia uses loops and waves rather than angular cuts.

## Style Requirements
- **Color:** Place at least three visibly contrasting, saturated hues in the composition.
- **Flowing form:** Bend one or more recognizable objects into continuous organic curves.
- **Contours:** Repeat sinuous lines with a visible rhythm instead of isolated decorative curls.
- **Optical space:** Distort a depth cue, horizon or recognizable silhouette using ripples or spirals.
- **Lettering:** If words appear, bend or crowd their letterforms into the visual rhythm.
- **Surface:** Preserve some analog print grain, imperfect ink edge or imperfect registration.
- **Mood:** Make the image feel wondrous or disorienting through its visible spatial and color effects.
