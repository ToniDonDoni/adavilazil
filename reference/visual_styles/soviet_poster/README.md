# Soviet Constructivist Poster

## Overview
A graphic-poster interpretation of Soviet Constructivism in the 1920s. The movement treated art as a tool for public communication; advertising and film posters combined dynamic geometry, photography and strong directional composition. The print effects below describe a modern reconstruction.

## Influenced By
- Russian avant-garde geometric abstraction: simplified forms and an emphasis on constructed composition.
- Revolutionary-era visual and mass-media experimentation: a functional role for design, photography and public messages.

## Influenced To
- Modern promotional graphic design: dynamic diagonal layouts, bold color and emblematic imagery continued to influence advertising abroad.

## Relations
- [German Expressionist poster](../german_poster/README.md): both compress space and create urgency; Constructivism favors controlled geometry over visibly carved distortion.
- [Psychedelic poster](../psychedelic_poster/README.md): both seek immediate poster impact, but Constructivism prizes directional structure while psychedelia bends legibility and perspective.

## Style Requirements
- **Value:** Use broad, flat light and dark blocks rather than gradual modeled lighting.
- **Palette:** Make red, black and cream dominant.
- **Geometry:** Construct the main focal elements from clearly recognizable triangles, circles or bars.
- **Direction:** Use at least one pronounced diagonal to establish movement.
- **Layering:** Flatten depth into visibly overlapping graphic planes.
- **Montage:** Combine at least two visually distinct image or shape layers.
- **Typography:** When words are present, integrate bold type into the geometric arrangement.
- **Printed surface:** Include a visible halftone, grain or slightly misregistered patch.
- **Energy:** Convey action with cropping, shifts of scale or intersecting directions.
