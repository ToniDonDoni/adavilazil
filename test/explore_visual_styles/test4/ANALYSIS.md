# Style Analysis

The specification is authoritative for implementation and evaluation: [SPEC-002-RAVEN-CITY.md](SPEC-002-RAVEN-CITY.md).

The German and psychedelic style cards provide candidate requirements; the actual pass/fail checks are the spec's **Acceptance Criteria**, not a separate checklist of source-style requirements.

- **German Expressionism:** black shadow masses, gouged edges, angular distortion, perspective tension.
- **Soviet Constructivism:** flat black/red/cream graphic geometry as independent comparator.
- **Psychedelic posters:** sustained flowing contours, chromatic optical rhythm, warped space.
- **Selected hybrid:** German Expressionist construction + psychedelic inner movement.
- **Critical integration check:** color must follow the anatomy of feathers and distorted architecture, not float on top as a decorative layer.
