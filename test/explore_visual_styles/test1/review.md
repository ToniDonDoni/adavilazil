**Diagnosis:** v1 places psychedelic effects around a mostly rigid raven; key is tiny; city feels like detached backdrop.

**Targeted revision:** merge ribbons with feather anatomy, curve architectural lines, add cut marks, enlarge the key and asymmetrically anchor the composition.

**Next iteration:** if strict woodcut texture and psychological tension are essential, vary edge roughness and deepen lighting before calling the style complete.
