# Test 1: v2 design prompt

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES
**Skill version used:** unversioned (legacy, prior to 1.0.0)

Create a hand-printed experimental **vector poster** of a black raven holding an **oversized antique golden key**. Fuse German Expressionist woodcut and 1960s psychedelic poster design through one shared distortion mechanism: the raven's angular feathers **turn into multicolored organic ribbons within its wing and torso**, and those same ribbons bend surrounding city architecture. Never place the styles in separate foreground/background layers. Hard black shadow, magenta, violet and burning gold; asymmetric geometry and a vortex. Add imperfect carved cuts and paper grain. Emotional tone: wonder mixed with anxiety. The raven and key must remain recognizable. Text: DRAMA TRIP.

**Corrections to v1:** make key large and legible; embed colored currents in wing anatomy; warp buildings; increase cut marks; avoid isolated decorative swirls.
