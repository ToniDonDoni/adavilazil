# Test 1: Drama Trip raven

**Subject:** a black raven clutching an antique golden key.

**Prompt:**

Create an original experimental art poster. Blend early German Expressionist woodcut and 1960s psychedelic poster design into **one** visual language. A black raven holds a golden key before a jagged, unstable city. Its carved, angular wings turn into continuous magenta, violet and golden ribbons **inside the same anatomy**. Sharp shadows and rough gouged ink marks evolve into fluid contours; the city and sky bend into a vortex. Antique paper, distressed analog printing, asymmetrical composition. Emotion: fascination, unease and wonder at once. Title: DRAMA TRIP. No comic-book look, no pasted-on psychedelic background, no heroic pose.

**Review:** inspect the transitions between angular and curved shapes, not just the palette.
