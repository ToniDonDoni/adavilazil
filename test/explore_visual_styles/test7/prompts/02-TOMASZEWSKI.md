# Prompt 02: Tomaszewski Individual Study

Create a single 3:4 portrait film-poster image of the same fictional adult female TV presenter: blonde hair, grey eyes, dark tailored jacket and visible lapel microphone. Keep her recognizable and make the on-air context clear.

Use Henryk Tomaszewski's artist-specific Polish film-poster language: reduce the subject to one concise visual metaphor rather than illustrating a literal studio scene. Use one large dark silhouette, one expressive vermilion brush gesture suggesting a voice or broadcast signal, asymmetrical framing, hand-drawn accent marks, purposeful color and strong readability from a distance. Keep the face unobstructed. Use a restrained cream, black, ochre and vermilion palette. Lettering may be minimal and hand-integrated; avoid generic retro decoration, sterile corporate layout, photorealism and excessive detail.
