# Prompt 03: Hybrid V1

Create one cohesive 3:4 portrait poster of the same blonde, grey-eyed female TV presenter with a visible lapel microphone. Combine the spatial language of Hiroshige's One Hundred Famous Views of Edo with Henryk Tomaszewski's concise, metaphorical poster composition.

Use Hiroshige for the cropped blossom branch, layered city, river and mountain view, oblique bridge, crisp woodblock contours, flat printed colors and selective sky and water bokashi. Use Tomaszewski for a dominant dark silhouette, asymmetrical poster hierarchy and a broad vermilion hand-painted gesture suggesting the presenter's voice travelling outward. Keep the palette restrained and preserve the presenter as the focal subject. This first implementation places the red gesture as a separate curved shape above and behind the presenter; the review will test whether that visual relationship reads as an integrated metaphor or detached decoration.
