# Prompt 01: Hiroshige Individual Study

Create a single 3:4 portrait print of the fixed subject: a fictional adult female TV presenter with blonde hair, grey eyes, a dark tailored jacket and a visible lapel microphone. Keep the same face and hair silhouette used in the other studies.

Interpret the scene through recurring visual devices in Hiroshige's One Hundred Famous Views of Edo: an oversized cherry-blossom branch cropped by the upper-left image edge; an oblique view over a river and bridge; a layered city skyline and distant mountain; crisp dark woodblock contours; distinct flat printed color areas; selective bokashi in sky and water; and a light seasonal rain. The presenter remains the main readable subject in the foreground. Use indigo, muted blue-green, cream, ochre and vermilion. Add restrained printed-paper grain. Avoid watercolor softness, anime styling, photorealism and arbitrary grunge. Include a small vertical ON AIR cartouche.
