# V3 Visual Review: Kuindzhi x Lalique

## Purpose and basis

Compare the [rendered V3 page](index-v3.html) with the [V3 SVG reference](images/SPEC-009-ABYSSAL-GARDEN-KUINDZHI-LALIQUE-V3.svg) and the [artistic specification](SPEC-009-ABYSSAL-GARDEN-KUINDZHI-LALIQUE-V3.md). These are implementer observations from actual page views and movement through the scene; they are not an independent art review.

The analytic chain is **artistic method -> color/composition decision -> visible result -> remaining difference**. The page remains a navigable interpretation of the illustration.

## Observed results

| Artistic method or decision | Visible result | Remaining difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kuindzhi: dominant dark field | Opening view retains blue-black water, dark canyon margins and open space around the light. | The procedural geology is more faceted and differently arranged than the drawn canyon. |
| Kuindzhi: one dominant cool source | A small bright nucleus has a broad mint inner halo and a wider teal atmosphere fading into darkness. | The nucleus is smaller and whiter than the SVG dot. |
| Continuous path of light | Soft connected shafts descend through the central channel, with feathered boundaries. | Their directions and spread follow 3D depth rather than the SVG's illustrated triangular paths. |
| Light as the primary focus | Surface illumination, bubbles and incidental glints remain subordinate in the opening composition. | Approaching the source enlarges the luminous region in the viewport. |
| Lalique: localized enamel and fine gold | Selected organic fans carry bounded teal/violet/rose regions, narrow gold ribs and a few pearl accents. | Close-up surfaces remain more opaque and faceted than the reference's enamel treatment. |
| Central focus with depth and negative space | The opening light sits near half width and 43 percent height; ship, sculpture and plants frame an open central passage. | Subject scale and placement differ from the illustration. |
| Recognizable underwater subjects | Wreck, stone fluke, fish, manta, kelp and canyon remain present while moving through the scene. | The wreck shows less damage and the fluke can still read as a plant-like form. |

## Comparison approach

Open the page and SVG at a similar landscape size. Compare the opening view for the relative size of the nucleus, the mint-to-teal falloff, shaft softness, dark-water dominance and distribution of enamel/gold. Then move toward and across the light to inspect depth, overlap and local material appearance.

The broad-halo and soft-shaft treatment is visibly closer to the SVG than the earlier small-halo, hard-cone page. Remaining differences in subject geometry and enamel finesse stay explicit; no complete reproduction of the illustration is claimed.
