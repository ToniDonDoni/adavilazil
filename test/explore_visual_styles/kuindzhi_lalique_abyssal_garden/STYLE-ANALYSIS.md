# Style Analysis: Kuindzhi, Lalique, Norstein

## Candidate selection

All three were present as unchecked candidates in [Task 0001](../../../tasks/0001-FUTURE-STYLE-LIST.md), and none had a reference entry in this repository before this change.

| Candidate | Observable method | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FS-153 Arkhip Kuindzhi, *Moonlit Night on the Dnieper* | Narrow cool light against vast dark pictorial space, connected reflection, subdued landscape | Selected |
| FS-225 René Lalique, Art Nouveau enamel jewellery | Opalescent organic forms, gilded contours and gem-like accents | Selected |
| FS-209 Yuri Norstein and Francheska Yarbusova, *Hedgehog in the Fog* | Cut-paper depth, fog occlusion and cinematic softness | Independent comparison, not in live blend |

## Compatible properties

Kuindzhi defines a global light hierarchy; Lalique defines a distinct local material treatment. The material can glow without destroying the nocturnal world if bright areas remain sparse. Both are testable through controlled light, color and form.

## Conflicts and resolutions

- **Light vs glitter:** cool moonlight stays primary; gold filigree and pearl glints are restricted to selected plants or moving creatures.
- **Jewellery vs geology:** opal colors appear only in fantasy biota; rocks, canyon and wreck stay matte and substantially darker.
- **Still painting vs camera movement:** source direction and global palette stay coherent while the viewer travels; specular response changes with angle.
- **Fog vs material readability:** distant color absorbs gently; near enamel surfaces retain edge/vein contrast.
- **Norstein's haze:** useful as a separate artistic study, but using it explicitly as a hybrid influence would violate the required two-artist selection.

## Concrete adaptation

[Kuindzhi style rules](../../../reference/visual_styles/kuindzhi_moonlit_dnieper/README.md): darkness, one source, beam, negative space. [Lalique](../../../reference/visual_styles/lalique_dragonfly_jewellery/README.md): hue-changing enamel, bounded gold filigree, pearls and matte contrast. [Norstein](../../../reference/visual_styles/norstein_hedgehog_fog/README.md) is a separate study with paper haze/occlusion.

[Composition rules](../../../engineering/COMPOSITION-RULES.md): focal hierarchy, leading light, three-layer depth, small fauna against imposing canyon. [Color rules](../../../engineering/COLOR-RULES.md): dominant blue-black environment, cold mint focal point and secondary small warm gold; contrast controls visual weight.

These are editorial translations of historical properties into procedural digital art, not reconstructions of original paintings, jewels or films.
