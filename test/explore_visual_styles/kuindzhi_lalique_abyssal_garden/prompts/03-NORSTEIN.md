# Exact study brief: Norstein

Create the same underwater fantasy subject as an original editable 1200 x 800 SVG made of depth-separated cut-paper-like shapes, atmospheric translucent strips, mottled surfaces and partial fog occlusions. The ship, sculpture and a small marine animal remain identifiable while distant structures disappear into mist. This is a study of multiplane depth and Franceska Yarbusova/Norstein collaboration; do not depict Hedgehog in the Fog characters or trace film frames.
