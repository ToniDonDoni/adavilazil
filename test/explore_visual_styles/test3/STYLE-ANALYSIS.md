# Style Analysis

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES 2.0.0

Subject: pine beside a lake at dusk, distant hills and moon.

| Style | Style Requirements evaluated | Fit for subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Levitan-inspired watercolor](../../../reference/visual_styles/levitan_watercolor/README.md) | LW-01 through LW-08 | Muted atmosphere, soft distance and quiet water |
| [German Expressionist poster](../../../reference/visual_styles/german_poster/README.md) | GE-01 through GE-08 | Sharp pine silhouette, tense diagonal |
| [Psychedelic poster](../../../reference/visual_styles/psychedelic_poster/README.md) | PP-01 through PP-08 | Liquid contours in tree and reflections |

**Selected pair:** Levitan + psychedelic. **Shared mechanism:** both let the environment carry emotion through color and space.

**Compatible:** LW-05 + PP-05 (depth becomes gently warped), LW-06 + PP-06 (quiet broad area carries wave rhythm), LW-07 + PP-07 (analog surface).

**Conflict:** LW-02 favors muted color; PP-02 favors saturated hues. Resolution: most of the painting stays muted, while saturated colors appear in narrow currents **inside the tree and its reflection**.

**Conflict:** LW-04 avoids rigid outlines; PP-04 uses continuous contours. Resolution: fluid contours exist selectively inside the trunk and water while distant hills remain unoutlined.
