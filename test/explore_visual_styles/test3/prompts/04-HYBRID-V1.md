# Dusk Current, hybrid v1

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES 2.0.0

A lone leaning pine beside a lake at dusk, distant hills and the moon reflected in water. Portrait art print, no text or people.

Apply [every combined Style Requirement H-01 through H-09](../RULES.md). Preserve diffuse atmospheric watercolor and broad muted blue-green washes. Inside the pine trunk, shift angular segments into fluid violet and magenta currents; let those same currents enter the lake reflection, carrying a rhythmic path toward a warm orange moon. Bend only the reflected horizon; preserve faded distant hills and softly edged washes. Visible paper tooth and irregular pigment. Mood: contemplative with slight perceptual unease, not a colorful filter placed over a watercolor.
