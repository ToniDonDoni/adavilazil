# Dusk Current: hybrid v2

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES 2.1.0

Same subject and canvas as v1: one leaning pine on a lake at dusk, distant hills, moon and its reflection; no text or people.

Apply **H-01 through H-09**, prioritizing failed/partial H-03 to H-08. Keep broad diffuse, low-contrast watercolor sky and hills. Make the pine trunk **physically transform** from muted angular bark into thin flowing magenta/violet/orange contours *within* its anatomy; carry those contours through branches and into a recognizably pine-shaped water reflection. Curve the lake's horizon subtly in sympathy with the tree. Create a continuous visual line from the trunk, through its mirrored trunk and rippling water, toward the reflected moon. Make pigment edges broken and washy over uneven paper grain; no smooth decorative neon ribbon pasted across the image. Keep at least two thirds muted. Convey quiet melancholy with subtle disorientation. Pine, hills, lake and moon must all remain legible.
