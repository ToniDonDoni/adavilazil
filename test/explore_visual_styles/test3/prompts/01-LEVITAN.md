# Levitan-inspired baseline

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES 2.0.0

Fixed subject: a lone leaning pine beside a calm lake at dusk, distant hills and a moon with its reflection; portrait art print, no text, no people.

Generate according to LW-01 through LW-08 in [the Levitan reference](../../../../reference/visual_styles/levitan_watercolor/README.md): soft diffuse twilight; predominantly muted blue-grays and earth greens; soft distant hills; no rigid landscape outlines; atmospheric depth; open sky and lake; translucent watercolor washes, visible paper tooth; contemplative stillness. Do not add saturated psychedelic contours.
