# Historical Style Requirements Snapshot: Test 3

These were the source Style Requirements used by the original [test3 specification](SPEC-001-LAKE-AT-DUSK.md) and reviews with skill 2.1.0. This snapshot preserves the meaning of historical LW/GE/PP IDs after the live catalog adopts flexible, unnumbered requirements. It does not impose IDs on new style cards.

## Levitan-Inspired Watercolor (LW)

- **LW-01 Light:** Use soft diffuse daylight or dusk without sharp cast shadows.
- **LW-02 Color:** Keep most of the scene in muted blue-gray and earth-green tones.
- **LW-03 Form:** Soften distant silhouettes; sharpen only selected foreground details.
- **LW-04 Line:** Avoid continuous hard outlines around landscapes.
- **LW-05 Space:** Show atmospheric depth by reducing distant contrast.
- **LW-06 Composition:** Preserve a broad, quiet sky or water area.
- **LW-07 Texture:** Show translucent overlapping washes and visible paper grain.
- **LW-08 Mood:** Convey stillness or melancholy through sparse detail and calm light.

## German Expressionist Poster (GE)

- **GE-01 Light:** Use large, hard-edged black shadows.
- **GE-02 Color:** Limit the palette to black, paper tones and at most one accent.
- **GE-03 Form:** Distort at least one recognizable object into sharp angles.
- **GE-04 Line:** Show jagged, carved-looking strokes inside the subject.
- **GE-05 Space:** Tilt or compress architectural or landscape perspective.
- **GE-06 Composition:** Build tension with a dominant diagonal.
- **GE-07 Texture:** Show visibly uneven ink or woodcut grain.
- **GE-08 Mood:** Convey unease using pose, shadow or spatial pressure.

## Psychedelic Poster (PP)

- **PP-01 Light:** Use contrasting colored light zones rather than neutral shading.
- **PP-02 Color:** Place at least three saturated, visibly contrasting hues.
- **PP-03 Form:** Bend recognizable shapes into fluid organic curves.
- **PP-04 Line:** Use repeating, continuous wave-like contours.
- **PP-05 Space:** Distort depth with ripples, spirals or warped perspective.
- **PP-06 Composition:** Carry the viewer's eye along a repeating visual rhythm.
- **PP-07 Texture:** Show analog print grain or imperfect color registration.
- **PP-08 Mood:** Suggest wonder or sensory disorientation through the distortion.
