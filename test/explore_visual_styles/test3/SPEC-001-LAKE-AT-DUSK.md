# SPEC-001: Lake at Dusk, Dusk Current

**Skill:** EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES 2.1.0

## 1. Problem and Current State
- Four reference styles have numbered Style Requirements, but this test has no rendered outputs yet.

## 2. Required State
- Produce three studies of the **same** subject according to the Levitan, German Expressionist and psychedelic Style Requirements.
- Produce a hybrid of Levitan-inspired watercolor and 1960s psychedelic poster art, then revise it after inspection.
- **Subject:** one leaning pine beside a lake at dusk, distant hills, moon and its reflection. Portrait art print, no text or people.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] **B-01:** Three baseline artworks are saved; each is audited against its source's numbered Style Requirements.
- [ ] **H-01 / LW-01:** Sky and hills use diffuse, shadowless dusk lighting.
- [ ] **H-02 / LW-02 + PP-02:** At least two-thirds of the scene remains muted, with narrow saturated accents.
- [ ] **H-03 / LW-03 + PP-03:** The pine's angular trunk or branches transform into flowing contours **within their anatomy**.
- [ ] **H-04 / LW-04 + PP-04:** Curves link pine and reflection; distant hills avoid hard outlines.
- [ ] **H-05 / LW-05 + PP-05:** Reflections warp space, while distant hills remain lower contrast.
- [ ] **H-06 / LW-06 + PP-06:** Open sky and lake preserve a readable rhythmic path toward the reflected moon.
- [ ] **H-07 / LW-07 + PP-07:** Transparent washes, paper grain and uneven printed pigment are visible.
- [ ] **H-08 / LW-08 + PP-08:** The scene is quiet at first glance but subtly unsettling up close.
- [ ] **H-09:** Pine, lake, distant hills, moon and reflection are identifiable.
- [ ] **V-01:** Save v1 and v2 prompts and artworks; score every H requirement with visual evidence after each rendering.

## 4. Analysis
- [Style comparison](STYLE-ANALYSIS.md): both styles communicate feeling through atmosphere and spatial deformation.
- **Compatible:** LW-05 / PP-05 (depth), LW-06 / PP-06 (rhythm), LW-07 / PP-07 (surface).
- **Conflict:** LW-02 muted palette vs PP-02 saturation. Keep a muted majority with colored currents inside the subject.
- **Conflict:** LW-04 soft edges vs PP-04 continuous contours. Limit hard contours to trunk and water.

## 5. Proposed Solution
- Follow each reference's Style Requirements for the three baselines.
- Apply [combined Style Requirements](RULES.md) to the hybrid; inspect v1, revise failed requirements, then inspect v2.
- Archive all design prompts, editable SVG illustrations and scored reviews in this test folder.
