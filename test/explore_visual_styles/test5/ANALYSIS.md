# Style Analysis

The specification is authoritative for implementation and evaluation: [SPEC-003-TV-PRESENTER.md](SPEC-003-TV-PRESENTER.md).

The three style cards provide candidate requirements; the actual pass/fail checks are the spec's **Acceptance Criteria**, not a separate checklist of source-style requirements.

- **Swiss Style:** modular grid, one sans-serif family, two or three flat colors, geometric primitives, asymmetric balance, generous empty field.
- **Fukuda negative space:** two values, at most three shapes, a reversible figure-ground reading, no texture.
- **Kruger montage:** achromatic image layer, white bold italic type on solid red bars, a bar crossing the subject, a second-person declaration.
- **Selected hybrid:** Swiss grid + Kruger bars. The grid supplies the column logic that makes the bars look placed; Kruger supplies the human subject the grid lacks.
- **Critical integration check:** every bar must sit on a grid column and at least one must cross the presenter. A bar in the margin is decoration, not composition.

**Subject constraints:** blonde hair and grey eyes are part of the Required State, so they are checked in AC-01 rather than left to taste. Two of the three references cannot carry them: the Fukuda study is deliberately two-value, and the Swiss palette is limited, which is why AC-01 and AC-02 are expected to land at partial rather than pass.
