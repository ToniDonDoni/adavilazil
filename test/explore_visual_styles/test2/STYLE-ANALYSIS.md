# Style analysis (before generation)

**Skill: EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES | Version: 1.0.0**

| Feature | German Expressionist woodcut | Soviet Constructivist poster | Psychedelic poster, 1960s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Violent chiaroscuro | Flat graphic tonal masses | Vibrating contrasts |
| Color | Cream/black, limited accent | Red, black, cream | Saturated magenta, orange, violet |
| Form | Angular distorted anatomy | Triangles, circles, hard diagonals | Fluid, melting organic contours |
| Line | Jagged, carved, irregular | Ruler-straight, clean | Curving and continuous |
| Space | Tilted oppressive city | Flattened layered photomontage | Warped and optically unstable |
| Composition | Psychological tension, sharp thrust | Dynamic diagonal propaganda geometry | Rhythmic, dense ornamental flow |
| Texture | Rough gouged print grain | Halftone, ink registration | Screenprint grain, repetition |
| Emotion | Unease, alienation | Urgency, collective momentum | Wonder, sensory expansion |

Origins and search terms: [German](../../../reference/visual_styles/german_poster/), [Soviet](../../../reference/visual_styles/soviet_poster/), [Psychedelic](../../../reference/visual_styles/psychedelic_poster/).

**Selection for hybrid:** German + psychedelic. Shared principle: both use deformation to communicate an emotion rather than to reproduce optics. Conflict is jagged geometry versus liquid flow. Integrate them *inside the same raven, key and architecture*. Soviet remains a comparison baseline.
