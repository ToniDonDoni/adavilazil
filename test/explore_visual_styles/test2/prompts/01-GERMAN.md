# German Expressionist baseline

**Skill: EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES | Version: 1.0.0**

Create a portrait 3:4 art poster with one **black raven clutching an oversized antique golden key** above a jagged city skyline. Use strictly early German Expressionist WOODCUT rules: bold cream-black chiaroscuro, violently angular wing planes, carved irregular contours and rough hand-printed ink. The city presses inward; unnerving dramatic shadows, raw emotional tension. Restrained cream-black with one muted vermilion accent. No psychedelic swirls, no modern gradients, no captions, no extra birds. The key must remain visibly golden. Pure standalone style study.
