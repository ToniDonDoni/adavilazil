# Soviet Constructivist baseline

**Skill: EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES | Version: 1.0.0**

Create a portrait 3:4 art poster with one **black raven clutching an oversized antique golden key** above a city skyline. Use strictly Soviet Constructivist poster language of the 1920s: sharp red-black-cream geometry, high-energy diagonals, triangular planes, cropped photomontage-like blocks, minimal halftone textures, flat graphic space and dynamic public-poster composition. Raven, gold key and city stay instantly identifiable. No psychedelic swirls, no expressionist brushwork, no text, no extra birds. Pure standalone style study.
