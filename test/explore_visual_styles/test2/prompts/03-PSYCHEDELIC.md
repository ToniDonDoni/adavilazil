# 1960s Psychedelic baseline

**Skill: EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES | Version: 1.0.0**

Create a portrait 3:4 art poster with one **black raven clutching an oversized antique golden key** above a city skyline. Use strictly 1960s psychedelic concert-poster visual language: liquid organic outlines, rippling and optically warped architecture, dense rhythmic curves, vivid violet/magenta/orange/yellow, irregular analog screenprint grain and hypnotic movement. Raven and key readable, dreamlike, wondrous and fluid. No expressionist carved marks, no Soviet diagonals, no text, no extra birds. Pure standalone style study.
