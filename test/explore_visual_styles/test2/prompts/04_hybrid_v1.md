# Drama Trip hybrid v1

**Skill: EXPLORE-VISUAL-STYLES | Version: 1.0.0**

Portrait 3:4 art poster: one black raven holds an oversized antique golden key above an unstable city. Invent one fused graphic language from German Expressionist woodcut and 1960s psychedelic poster art. Both traditions distort reality for emotional impact. Jagged black carved feather planes gradually soften into magenta/violet/orange flowing ribbons **inside the same wings and neck**. Deep hard shadows, irregular gouged-line print texture; city towers bend in the same current as the bird. Asymmetric dramatic composition linking the raven's eye, bright key and warped skyline. Emotion: fascinating and slightly frightening, neither a happy trip nor horror. Handmade, distressed analog ink. No text, no pasted-on swirls, no isolated background effects, no extra birds. Raven and key legible.
