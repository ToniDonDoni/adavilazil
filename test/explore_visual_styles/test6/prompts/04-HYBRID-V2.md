# Prompt 04: Hybrid V2 Revision

Revise Hybrid V1 without changing the fixed subject or the two selected style references. Keep the blonde, grey-eyed TV presenter and the Hiroshige-inspired portrait landscape: cropped blossom branch, distant city and mountain, oblique river bridge, flat woodblock color planes, crisp contours and selective atmospheric gradation.

Fix the specific V1 failure: the vermilion Tomaszewski-style gesture must no longer float above the presenter. Start it at the lapel microphone and cable, carry it across the lower jacket and continue it into a bridge-like curve over the river, so the voice visually becomes a bridge across the city. Keep the ribbon below the eyes and face. Use a few irregular dry-brush accents, not many separate ornaments. Preserve the concise poster hierarchy and controlled indigo, cream, ochre and vermilion palette. Do not turn the result into a triptych or collage.
