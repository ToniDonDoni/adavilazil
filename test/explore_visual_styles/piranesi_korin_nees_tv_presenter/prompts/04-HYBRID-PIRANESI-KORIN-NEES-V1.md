Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original finished artwork, not a mockup of a printed page. Subject: one fictional adult female television presenter, blonde short chin-length bob, visibly light gray irises (not blue, green, brown), no resemblance to a named celebrity. Create one integrated surreal broadcast scene, not a collage, contact sheet, triptych, comparison board or separate tiles. Dense irregular crosshatching and visibly incised detail, dramatic black structural masses against pale distant paper. The blonde gray-eyed host stands center-lower behind her broadcast desk, face large enough to inspect but dwarfed by rising piers. The low desk's FRONT FASCIA is a flat gold-leaf panel carrying Korin-like repeating blue rounded iris blossoms and long green blade leaves, unequal clumps and exposed gold gaps; the flowers must be painted decoration ON the desk, not a bouquet standing above it. At upper right, physically mounted between the architectural supports, a complete FLAT PORTRAIT STUDIO DISPLAY contains a Schotter-inspired field on pale paper: many equal-size closed unfilled square outlines, near-aligned top gradually becoming BOTH displaced and rotated downward, with gaps and overlaps, uniformly thin dark strokes, all contained inside a clear margin. The display must not be warped by perspective or blocked by cables. The gold botanical desk, tall square-field display, microphone, cue card, and tripod camera belong to the same studio around the same host. No geometric overlays on the face, no flowers floating in space, no colored light on the architecture. Gray irises, no blue eyes.
