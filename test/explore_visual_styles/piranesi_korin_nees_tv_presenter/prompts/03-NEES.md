```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original finished artwork, not a mockup of a printed page. Subject: one fictional adult female television presenter, blonde short chin-length bob, visibly light gray irises (not blue, green, brown), no resemblance to a named celebrity. She holds a recognizable handheld microphone in her right hand near her chest and one plain blank cue card in her left hand. She wears a tailored jacket and stands behind a low broadcast desk. A small professional TV camera on a tripod points toward her from one side, establishing a television studio. No other people. Her blonde hair must remain visibly pale; her eyes must be large and clear enough to inspect in the full-resolution image. No letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, captions, or readable text. Her facial expression is composed and attentive. Portrait orientation, approximately 2:3. A minimal monochrome editorial line illustration in a television studio. The host is in the lower left/center, front-facing with visible neutral gray eyes and light paper-colored blonde hair outlined delicately. Her body, low desk, microphone, cue card and small tripod camera must remain recognizable. The design centerpiece is one complete tall, light rectangular studio display occupying the right half; no presenter or prop overlaps its inner drawing. Within that display ONLY, use a Schotter-inspired field of many equal-size CLOSED UNFILLED SQUARE OUTLINES. At top they form a nearly regular rectangular grid; toward bottom BOTH positions and rotation angles vary progressively and locally, producing gaps and intersections. Constant square size, thin dark line, no filled squares, no cubes, no perspective distortion, no triangles, no gradients. All squares completely inside a clear pale margin on every side. The field remains taller than wide. This is a visibly separate flat screen inside the studio, not a pattern superimposed on her face. Outside the screen use simple consistent black line illustration on cream paper, without architectural grandeur or flowers. Do not draw thick dark rims on the screen.
```
